What Edvance does

Three jobs, done together

Mapping, consistency, and mastery are one system. Each feeds the next, so a flagged contradiction changes your revision list.

Assessment-to-source mapping

Every question is wired to the lesson, slide, and transcript segment where its answer was taught. “What do I study?” stops being guesswork.

01

Course consistency intelligence

Contradictions between slide decks, transcripts, and notes are surfaced as flagged inconsistencies — not silent traps.

02

Per-concept mastery

Results update mastery at the concept and question level, so your revision list is built from evidence instead of vibes.

Why it exists

The four questions Edvance answers

Assessment intelligence only matters if it resolves real exam uncertainty.

01

What exactly am I expected to know?

Every assessment maps to a defined concept set, made explicit up front.

02

Where was it taught?

Each question links back to the exact lesson, slide, and timestamp.

03

Do the materials agree?

Sources that contradict each other or the assessment get flagged.

04

What have I not mastered yet?

Concepts scored by evidence, so the next revision is obvious.
