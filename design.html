Brand & interface sheet

An artisan-poster palette — deep teal for structure, cream for the page, rust for flags, sage for growth — with an italic serif display voice and printing-press detailing. Mirrors app/globals.css; keep the two in sync.

Logo

Seal monogram + wordmark
Edvance Course Intelligence Edvance

The idea: an E built from three stacked source bars. The middle bar is rust because Edvance highlights the evidence that matters — and flags the source that disagrees. It reads at 16px, holds up as an app icon, and never needs a tagline to explain itself.

Lockups: horizontal (mark left, wordmark right) for headers and footer; mark alone for favicons and collapsed chrome; wordmark alone only above 24px. Minimum clear space equals the height of the mark's middle bar. Never rotate, outline, or re-colour the mark outside this palette.
